Who We Are

Kazakhstan's Premier Petroleum Refinery

Located in the heart of Central Asia's energy corridor, Siberia Refinery operates one of the region's most advanced petroleum refining facilities. Our 150,000 barrels-per-day capacity serves customers across 30+ countries, delivering premium fuels that power industries, transportation, and economies worldwide.

We combine decades of refining expertise with cutting-edge technology to produce petroleum products that meet the most rigorous international specifications.

  • Founded in 1999 by Mikhail Kulibayev
  • 5,000+ consecutive safety days
  • 1,200+ highly skilled employees
  • $2.5B facility investment

Our Journey

25 Years of Growth

1999

Foundation

Siberia Refinery established in Almaty with initial capacity of 50,000 barrels/day.

2005

First Export

Began international exports with first Jet A-1 shipment to European aviation clients.

2012

Major Expansion

$1.2B expansion doubled capacity to 100,000 barrels/day. ISO 9001 certification achieved.

2018

Euro 5 Upgrade

Completed ultra-low sulfur diesel conversion meeting EN590 Euro 5 standards.

2024

150K Milestone

Reached 150,000 barrels/day capacity, serving 30+ countries globally.
