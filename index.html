About Us

A Legacy of Refining Excellence Since 1999

Siberia Refinery is Kazakhstan's premier petroleum refining facility, operating at the intersection of innovation and tradition. Our state-of-the-art facility produces premium fuels that meet the most stringent international standards including ASTM, EN, and ISO certifications.

  • ISO 9001:2015 & ISO 14001 Certified
  • ASTM D1655 Jet A-1 Production
  • Euro 5 EN590 Diesel Standard
  • 24/7 Operations & Global Logistics
